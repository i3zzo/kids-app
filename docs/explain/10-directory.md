# The Explore directory now has 361 real places in Kuwait, special-needs support and events

## What changed for parents

Explore → Directory used to list 6 government hospitals. It now lists **361 places** across ten sections (new **Medical centres**, **Therapy & rehab** and **Support services** sections were added) and all six governorates. Explore also has a new **Events** tab. Each card shows:

- the name, governorate and area, plus the street address when a source gives one;
- the **price** when a public source shows it (for example "KD 2,400 a year" or "KD 14 entry"), or "Price not published";
- a short note about the price, such as "fees vary by grade, check with the school" or "from an undated blog";
- "From a public source checked on 21 November 2026";
- the existing warning, "Details not yet confirmed by our team".

The Map button now works for every place: it opens the place's own map link, or a Google Maps search for its name and area.

## Where the data came from

Nothing was made up. Every listing has its source link and the date it was checked, which is stored with the row and visible in the admin panel.

| Section | Main sources |
| --- | --- |
| Hospitals | e.gov.kw list of government hospitals (phone numbers), Wikipedia list of hospitals in Kuwait, UK government list of medical facilities, uae-medical-insurance.com, edarabia.com, zavis.ai |
| Medical centres | e.gov.kw list of every Ministry of Health primary health care clinic and specialised centre with its phone number (dental clinics left out), plus private polyclinics from uae-medical-insurance.com, edarabia.com, zavis.ai and Bupa |
| Paediatricians | The hospitals' own paediatrics pages (New Mowasat, Kuwait Hospital), Al Mulla Group (Royale Hayat), daleeeel.com, zavis.ai |
| Nurseries and schools | edarabia.com school and nursery lists (fees and addresses) |
| Activities | ootlah.com, kuwaitexpat.com, Kuwait Times, hilitehomes.com |
| Toy stores | LEGO and Alshaya store locators, Retail Times (Hamleys) |
| Kids' clothing | Alshaya store locators (Mothercare, Next), Al Tayer (Mamas & Papas) |

Rules I followed:

- **Prices** are entered only when a source shows them. School fees are the yearly figure edarabia shows (fees vary by grade). Nursery fees on edarabia don't say the period, so the app shows the amount without "a year". No hospital or clinic publishes consultation fees, so those say "not published".
- **Phone numbers** are copied only from a source, and only if they fit the +965 8-digit format. Short numbers like 1826666 were left out.
- Discount prices from deal sites (for example "Future Kid from 1.5 KD on ootlah") go in the note, not as the price.
- Mothercare stores that the store locator no longer lists (Rawda, Salwa, Mubarak Al-Kabeer/Qurain) were left out. A 2012 Toys R Us address was left out because I couldn't confirm the store is still open.
- Governorates follow the official areas (for example The Avenues in Al-Rai is in Farwaniya, South Surra and Salwa are in Hawalli).

## Special needs

Every section of the directory now has a **Special needs** switch. Turn it on to see only places that serve children with special needs. Then pick a need, such as Autism, Down syndrome, Hearing or Wheelchair access, to narrow the list. Places that say they serve "all disabilities" show up for every need. Each card says which needs the place serves.

There are two new sections: **Therapy & rehab** (speech, OT, physio, ABA and rehabilitation centres) and **Support services** (the four offices of the Public Authority for Disability Affairs, PADA).

Where the data came from:
- **The official PADA list on Kuwait Government Online.** It covers 31 nurseries, 39 schools and 13 centres for children with disabilities, with addresses, phone numbers and the disabilities each one serves. Two adult-only centres were left out. The page has no "last updated" date.
- **The PADA branches page** for the four offices.
- **The centres' own websites** for COMMUNICATE, BrainBox, ARGAN Riaya, Active Care and the KOC Child Development Unit (Ahmadi Hospital).
- **A Kuwait Times article (October 2025)**. It describes The Avenues and 360 Mall as built for accessibility, so the 14 places we list inside them are tagged "wheelchair access".
- **An undated special-needs guide on hilitehomes.com**, used for three entries only: the Applied Behavior Center, Kharafi Activity Kids Center (free for children with disabilities) and the therapy centre at Al-Sabah Hospital. The cards say the source is undated.

When the PADA list says a school has "inclusive or special-needs classes" but not which needs, the card says "Special needs (ask which)". It doesn't appear when a parent filters for one specific need, because we don't know which needs the school serves.

What's missing:
- **Prices.** None of the special-needs providers publishes fees. One therapy site shows KD 20 a session but gives no address, so it isn't listed. One school's fee table is from 2021–22, so we left it out.
- **Adaptive toys and clothing shops.** We found no shop in Kuwait with a public listing for these. The toy and clothing entries above are mainstream stores inside the accessible malls.
- **Paediatricians and medical centres** with developmental services. Only the KOC Child Development Unit and the Al-Sabah Hospital therapy centre have a source that says so.
- **Inclusive activities.** Kuwait Riding Center's Little Wings programme and Swing Kuwait (golf) are named in the undated guide, but it gives no location, so they aren't listed yet.

The team should call the PADA-listed nurseries and schools first. The government list has no date, and nurseries open and close often.

## Events and "New and trending"

Explore → Events shows two lists:

- **New and trending:** places with a "trending until" date in the admin panel, plus the reason (for example "New zones opened on 1 October 2026"). Today that is Al Shaheed Park's new Phase III and Pixel Run at Al Khiran Mall, both from dated news articles. The mark ends on its own date.
- **On now and coming up:** dated events, soonest first. An event disappears the day after it ends. Each event shows its dates, venue, hours, price when published, the source and the date it was checked.

There is only **one** event today: the 49th Kuwait International Book Fair (18–28 November 2026). Winter Wonderland's 2026/27 season hasn't announced its dates or prices yet, so it isn't listed, and most other upcoming events I found were adult concerts. Events go stale fast, so keeping this list useful needs a regular refresh (a weekly scheduled update can do the research and add new events as drafts for the team to publish).

Admin: a new **Kids' events** page, and three new Directory fields ("Show as New and trending until" and the reason in Arabic and English).

## What the team should do next

1. In the admin panel (Directory), open each place, call or visit, correct anything wrong, then set **Verified on**. The "not yet confirmed" warning disappears for that place.
2. Fill the gaps above. When you add a price, add the source link and the date you checked; the database refuses a price without them.
3. To change many rows at once, edit `supabase/directory/places.csv` and run `node scripts/directory-csv-to-sql.mjs` to produce a new migration. Rows are matched by their `ref`, so this updates places instead of adding copies, and keeps the team's status and "Verified on".

If you would rather parents only see places the team has confirmed, one line of SQL can set all unconfirmed places to "In review", which hides them until they are checked.

## Speed

Special-needs filter, at 20,000 listings with 1 in 10 tagged, as a visitor. "Any need" in a section takes 0.05–0.06 ms. One governorate takes 0.10–0.22 ms. "Autism" takes 0.30–0.65 ms. A need that no place has yet takes 4–7 ms, because it reads the whole section. A separate index on the tags made no difference in these runs, so we didn't add one (`supabase/perf/directory.sql`).


Measured on the local database with 20,000 published places (about 100× today), as a visitor so RLS is included, 3 runs each (`supabase/perf/directory.sql`):

| Directory query | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One section, one governorate, page 3 | uses the existing index | 0.09–0.16 ms (unchanged index) |
| One section, all governorates, page 3 | 5.6–7.2 ms (reads every row, then sorts) | 0.05–0.08 ms (new index) |
| One section, name search | – | 3.7–8.5 ms |
| Events: on now and coming up (20,000 events, most past) | 5.4 ms (seq scan) | 0.06–0.09 ms (new index) |

The new `places (category, name_en, id)` index serves "All governorates". Name search still scans the section; a trigram index would speed it up but adds write cost and an extension, and isn't needed at today's size. Pages are still 20 places, ordered by name then id. Nothing here was measured on a phone or with network throttling: the change adds 7 columns to the same single request.
